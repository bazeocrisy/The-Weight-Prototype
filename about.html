About

Why The Weight exists

A lot of men were taught to handle it alone. We think it’s easier to carry when you don’t have to.

The Weight is a welcoming, practical place for adult men to set down some of what they’re carrying. It’s for men from every background and stage of life: veterans and service members, men without stable housing, divorced fathers, caregivers, and men facing work, money, housing, or relationship problems.

What we are

A starting point: plain-language guidance, a private place to write things out, and links to organizations we’ve checked.

What we’re not

We aren’t a therapy provider, doctors, or an emergency service, and nobody monitors this site. If you need to talk to someone now, call or text 988 in the U.S. If someone is in immediate physical danger, call 911.

You’re not the only one carrying this (statistics)

How we handle information and privacy

We list resources only after verifying them and show when each was last checked. Resources marked “Suggested addition” were added for this prototype and still need The Weight’s own check.

This prototype has no accounts, no forms that send data, no cookies, no analytics, and no storage. The cards you pick in the questions are kept only in the open page and disappear when you leave or start over. What you type in Unload or Talk It Through never leaves your device.

Supporting the mission

Everything here is free. The Weight is an independent project, not a nonprofit, and contributions are not tax-deductible. See Support the mission on theweightmen.com for current details. The best way to help is to share the site with a man who could use it.
